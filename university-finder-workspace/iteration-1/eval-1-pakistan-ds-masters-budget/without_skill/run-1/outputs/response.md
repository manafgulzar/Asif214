# Master's in Data Science / AI for Fall 2027 on about $15,000 a year

Congratulations on finishing your BS! Your profile is a good fit for budget study abroad. A 3.2/4.0 CGPA and IELTS 6.5 meet the entry requirements for most of the programs below. $15,000 a year is roughly €13,000. That is enough to live and study in **Germany, Italy, Hungary, China, Taiwan or Malaysia** without a scholarship, or almost without one. It is not enough for the UK, the Netherlands, Ireland, Sweden or Finland unless you win a full scholarship.

It is October 2026, so **most deadlines fall between December 2026 and March 2027.** Start now.

> Fees, stipends and deadlines change every year. The figures below are approximate. Check each one on the university's official page before you apply.

## How to read your profile

| Item | Where you stand |
|---|---|

| Pakistani passport | Pakistanis do **not** need Germany's APS certificate. HEC is the channel for several government scholarships: Hungary, China and Korea. |

## Tier 1: Best fit for your budget (apply to several)

### Germany: no tuition at most public universities
You pay only a semester fee, about €150–350 per semester. For the visa you must show a **blocked account of €11,904 per year (€992/month)**, the 2026 figure, which may rise slightly for 2027. All-in, expect about €12,000–13,000 a year ($14–15k). You may legally work part-time, and many students earn €500–900 a month from student jobs.

### Italy: low fees plus regional "DSU" scholarships
Italian public universities charge fees based on family income. With a **DSU regional scholarship**, which is need-based and usually gives a full tuition waiver, about €6,000–8,000 a year in cash and sometimes a free canteen or housing, you can get close to zero net cost. Your family's $15k income profile is a strong fit for need-based aid. You will need an **ISEE parificato** (family income and asset documents legalized through the Italian embassy), so start collecting documents now.

|---|---|---|

**Deadlines:** many Italian universities have early windows from **November 2026 to March 2027** for non-EU applicants. Pre-enrollment on Universitaly and the visa process follow in spring and summer. DSU applications are usually due July–September 2027. **Caution:** Italian student-visa processing in Pakistan has had long delays, so apply in the earliest window you can.

### Hungary: Stipendium Hungaricum (fully funded, large Pakistan quota)
This is one of the most accessible full scholarships for Pakistani students. It covers **full tuition, a monthly stipend, a dormitory place or housing allowance, and medical insurance.** The stipend is modest (about HUF 43,700/month), so plan on topping it up with roughly $2,000–4,000 a year from your family. That fits your budget easily.

## Tier 2: Strong Asian options (cheap living, good scholarships)

### China: CSC (Chinese Government Scholarship) and university scholarships
This is fully funded: **tuition, accommodation, medical insurance and a stipend of about CNY 3,000/month.** Pakistani applicants are very welcome, and many programs are English-taught.
- **Tsinghua, Zhejiang University, Shanghai Jiao Tong, Harbin Institute of Technology, Beihang, Wuhan University, UESTC, Huazhong UST**: all have CS/AI/Data Science master's programs in English.
- Routes: **CSC Type A** (via HEC or the Chinese embassy) or **Type B** (apply directly to the university, often easier). Many universities also offer their own scholarships.
- **Deadlines:** usually **January–April 2027**. Contact potential supervisors early, because an acceptance letter from a professor boosts your chances a lot.

### Taiwan: university scholarships for international students
Many universities offer **tuition waivers plus a monthly stipend** (about NT$10,000–20,000) to international master's students. Living costs are low and AI/semiconductor research is strong.
- **National Tsing Hua University (NTHU), National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), National Taiwan University of Science & Technology (NTUST)**: English-taught CS/AI/Data Science master's programs.
- **Deadlines:** roughly **December 2026–March 2027** for fall intake.

### South Korea: research universities and GKS
- **KAIST, POSTECH, UNIST, GIST, DGIST**: international master's students usually receive **full tuition plus a monthly stipend** through scholarships or lab funding. These are competitive, and your projects and a strong statement of purpose matter more than CGPA. Fall 2027 rounds generally close in early 2027.
- **GKS (Global Korea Scholarship), graduate track:** the 2027 call is expected around **February–March 2027** (not yet announced). **Timing warning:** GKS normally starts with a **one-year Korean language course**, so your degree would begin around **Fall 2028** unless you already hold TOPIK level 5+.

### Malaysia: affordable without a scholarship (safety option)
A coursework master's lasts 1–1.5 years. Total tuition is about **RM 30,000–45,000 (about $7,000–10,000)** and living costs are about $5,000–6,000 a year. It fits your budget with no scholarship, entry is straightforward and the visa process is smooth.
- **Universiti Malaya (UM)**, M.Sc. Data Science / Applied Computing
- **Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)**, M.Sc. Data Science
- **Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM)**, Master of Data Science
- Applications are open almost year-round. Aim for **March–May 2027** for the September intake.

### Türkiye: Türkiye Burslari (fully funded)
This covers tuition, accommodation, a stipend, insurance, a flight and **a one-year Turkish language course**, so your degree would likely start in Fall 2028. Strong English-taught options include **METU (Ankara), Bilkent, Boğaziçi and ITU**. **Deadline:** usually around **20 February**.

## Tier 3: Stretch scholarships (apply to 2–3, they cost only time)

- **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's** (EU-funded, study in 2–3 European countries): covers full tuition plus **about €1,400/month** for 2 years. Relevant programs: **EMJM in Big Data Management & Analytics (BDMA), Data Science (EMDS), Artificial Intelligence / Intelligent Systems, Cybersecurity**, and others. Search the EACEA Erasmus Mundus catalogue. Deadlines are **mostly December 2026–February 2027**. These are very competitive at 3.2, so strong projects, research or work experience and an excellent motivation letter are essential.
- **Politecnico di Milano / Unibo / Padova merit scholarships**: listed above. Apply in the earliest window.
- **Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals**: fully funded, but they require about 3,000 hours of work experience and demonstrated leadership. Consider it only if you have work experience by the deadline (about February).

## Tips to strengthen a 3.2 CGPA application
- **Show your projects**: a GitHub with 2–3 solid ML/data projects, Kaggle work, or your final-year project written up well.
- **Explain your trajectory**: if your grades improved in later semesters, or your CS/math course grades are higher, say so in your SOP.
- **Use strong recommendation letters**, ideally from professors who supervised your project or research.
- **Consider retaking IELTS for 7.0** only if you target Erasmus Mundus or top-tier schools. 6.5 is enough for nearly everything else.
- **Don't pay agents for the free scholarships** (Stipendium Hungaricum, CSC, DSU, Erasmus Mundus). You can apply directly.

Good luck! With Germany, Italy and Hungary together you have a very realistic path to a quality Master's within your budget.
