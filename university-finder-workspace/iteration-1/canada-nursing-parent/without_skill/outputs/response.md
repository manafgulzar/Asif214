# Nursing (BScN) options for a Grade 12 Toronto student with a 91% average

*Prepared October 10, 2026, for Fall 2027 entry.*

## The short version

- **91% puts her in a strong position for most Ontario BScN programs.** It is about at the line for the most competitive ones (McMaster, Western, Queen's, TMU), where offers often go to students in the low-to-mid 90s. She should apply to a mix of schools.
- **The deadline that matters most is January 15, 2027.** That is the OUAC "equal consideration" date for current Ontario high school students. A few schools also need extra pieces, such as the **Casper test** or a supplementary form, and some of those are due on separate dates. Out-of-province schools have their own deadlines, mostly February 1 to March 1.
- **About "co-op" in nursing:** almost no Canadian BScN has a formal paid co-op like engineering does. Every accredited BScN instead has **required clinical placements**, usually starting in year 1 or 2. They end with a long final-year **"consolidation" or preceptorship**: several hundred hours working one-on-one with an RN. So the useful comparison is the *quality* of the placements: hospital partners, how early they start, and what specialty options there are.
- **Remember the Grade 12 prerequisites:** English 4U, Biology 4U, Chemistry 4U and a 4U math. Most schools take Advanced Functions, Calculus & Vectors or Data Management. Schools calculate her admission average from her **top 6 4U/M courses including those prerequisites**. That number can differ from her overall average, so work it out.

**A Toronto note:** the University of Toronto's Lawrence Bloomberg Faculty of Nursing does **not** admit students straight from high school. It is a 2-year accelerated BScN for people who already have university credits. It stays a backup route if she does an undergrad in another field first.

## Key dates and a suggested timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Now to November 2026** | Finalize a list of about 6 schools. Confirm her top-6 average with her guidance counsellor. Check whether she needs **Casper** (McMaster for sure) and book a test date early, because sittings fill up and results take weeks to reach schools. |
| **November to December 2026** | Submit the OUAC 101 application. Early is better than last-minute. Start any supplementary applications and scholarship forms; some schools have early scholarship deadlines in December. |
| **January 15, 2027** | **OUAC equal consideration deadline** for current Ontario high school students. |
| **About February 1, 2027** | Typical deadline for supplementary forms and documents at several Ontario schools, including Queen's nursing. Also the **McGill** deadline for Ontario applicants. Most Casper results need to be in by around this time. |
| **February to March 2027** | Out-of-province deadlines (Alberta round-based, Dalhousie, Memorial). Send midterm Grade 12 marks if a school asks for them. |
| **February to May 2027** | Offers roll out. Most Ontario decisions arrive by late May. |
| **June 1, 2027** | Usual Ontario date to accept an offer (confirm in OUAC). |
| **Summer 2027** | After accepting, nursing programs need **clinical clearance** before placements: immunizations, a vulnerable-sector police check, CPR/BLS and mask fit testing. Start early. |

## Our recommendation

A balanced list for her could look like:

- **Reach:** McMaster (with Casper), TMU (TMU site), Western, Queen's
- **Match:** Ottawa, McGill
- **Likely:** York, Ontario Tech, or TMU's Centennial site. These are close to home and still lead to a full BScN.

If clinical placements are her top priority, these questions are worth asking at fall open houses or virtual info sessions:

1. In what year do hospital placements start?
2. How many total clinical hours are there?
3. Can students choose their final-year consolidation specialty and location, such as ICU, paediatrics or ER?
4. What is the program's NCLEX-RN first-attempt pass rate?

## Caveats

- Admission averages change every year. The figures above combine official ranges (TMU, OUAC minimums) with crowdsourced data from Uniscope, which is self-reported. Use them as a rough guide only.
- Deadlines were checked as of October 2026, and several 2027 dates come from prior-year patterns or third-party summaries. **Confirm every date on ouac.on.ca and each school's admissions page** before relying on it.
