# Country Notes for Study Destinations

Quick orientation for the most common destinations. Figures are rough ranges for planning only — always confirm current numbers on official sites, since tuition and visa rules change yearly.

## United States
- **Applications:** Common App / Coalition (undergrad); direct per-school for grad. Early Action/Decision ~Nov 1–15; Regular ~Jan 1–15. Grad: Dec–Feb for Fall.
- **Tests:** SAT/ACT increasingly optional (check per school — some top schools reinstated it). TOEFL/IELTS/Duolingo for internationals. GRE often optional for master's.
- **Cost:** Public out-of-state/intl tuition ~$25k–45k; private ~$45k–65k; living ~$15k–25k.
- **Funding:** Need-blind for internationals at only a handful of schools; many privates offer need-based aid. Grad: RA/TA positions, especially PhDs (usually fully funded). Fulbright for many nationalities.
- **Visa:** F-1; OPT 1 year (+2 years STEM extension).

## United Kingdom
- **Applications:** UCAS (undergrad), 5 choices; Oxbridge/medicine deadline mid-Oct, others late Jan. Master's: direct, rolling.
- **Length:** Bachelor's 3 years (4 in Scotland); master's usually 1 year — big cost saver.
- **Cost:** Intl tuition ~£15k–40k; living ~£12k–18k (higher in London).
- **Funding:** Chevening (master's, fully funded), Commonwealth Scholarships, GREAT scholarships, university merit awards.
- **Visa:** Student visa; Graduate Route 2 years post-study (check current policy; has been under review).

## Canada
- **Applications:** Direct or provincial portals (OUAC for Ontario). Deadlines Jan–Mar.
- **Cost:** Intl tuition CAD 20k–60k; living CAD 15k–22k.
- **Funding:** Limited for intl undergrads (some entrance awards, Lester B. Pearson at UofT). Grad research degrees often funded.
- **Visa:** Study permit (provincial attestation letter caps since 2024); PGWP up to 3 years.

## Australia
- **Intakes:** Feb and Jul. Direct applications, rolling.
- **Cost:** Intl tuition AUD 30k–50k; living ~AUD 25k+ (visa minimum).
- **Funding:** Australia Awards (selected countries), Research Training Program for research degrees.

## Germany
- **Tuition:** Public universities mostly free (semester fee ~€150–400). Exception: Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students ~€1,500/semester; TUM charges non-EU tuition since 2024.
- **Applications:** Uni-Assist or direct; Winter intake deadline usually Jul 15 (Jan 15 for summer). Many English-taught master's.
- **Requirements:** Undergrad from some countries needs Studienkolleg / one year of university first. Blocked account ~€11,900+/year for visa.
- **Funding:** DAAD scholarships, Deutschlandstipendium.

## Netherlands
- **Applications:** Studielink; deadlines often Jan 15 (selective programs) to May 1.
- **Cost:** Non-EU tuition ~€9k–20k; living ~€12k–15k.
- **Funding:** NL Scholarship, Orange Tulip (some countries), university excellence scholarships.

## France
- **Cost:** Public universities for non-EU: ~€2,900 (bachelor's) / ~€3,900 (master's) per year; many universities waive down to EU rates. Grandes écoles/business schools much more.
- **Applications:** Campus France "Études en France" procedure for many nationalities.
- **Funding:** Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (master's/PhD).

## Nordics
- **Norway:** Tuition introduced for non-EU in 2023 at public universities — no longer free.
- **Sweden:** Non-EU tuition SEK 80k–200k; Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals. Apply via universityadmissions.se (mid-Jan).
- **Finland:** Non-EU tuition €8k–18k; many universities give 50–100% tuition waivers. Studyinfo.fi, Jan deadline.
- **Denmark:** Non-EU tuition €6k–16k; limited government scholarships.

## Ireland
- Intl tuition €10k–25k; Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship; 2-year Stamp 1G post-study (master's).

## Asia
- **Japan:** MEXT scholarship (fully funded, via embassy or university). National university tuition ~¥535,800/year. Growing English-taught programs (Global 30).
- **South Korea:** GKS (Global Korea Scholarship), fully funded incl. language year.
- **China:** CSC scholarship; tuition ~CNY 20k–40k.
- **Malaysia:** Low cost (tuition ~USD 3k–8k, living ~USD 4k–6k); branch campuses of UK/Australian universities.
- **Turkey:** Türkiye Burslari (fully funded); public university tuition low.
- **Taiwan:** Taiwan ICDF & MOE scholarships; tuition ~USD 3k–4k.

## Global scholarships
- **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's** — fully funded, study in 2–3 European countries, open to all nationalities.
- **Fulbright** (US), **Chevening** (UK), **Commonwealth** (UK, for Commonwealth citizens), **DAAD** (Germany), **MEXT** (Japan), **GKS** (Korea), **Türkiye Burslari**, **Swedish Institute**, **Australia Awards**, **Aga Khan Foundation** (selected developing countries, postgrad).
